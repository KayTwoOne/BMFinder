Welcome to BMFinder

BMFinder helps you save and recognise friends, teammates, and familiar Arma 3 players using BattleMetrics pages available in your browser.

Stored locally

Saved people, labels, server information, and recent observations are stored in this browser profile. BMFinder does not send them to a BMFinder server.

What BMFinder reads

When you visit supported BattleMetrics pages, BMFinder can read relevant player and server information needed to update people and servers you have chosen to save.

Intentional server checks

When you choose a refresh or enable live updates, BMFinder opens BattleMetrics pages sequentially using your existing browser session. Checks are deliberately paced.

Private profiles

BMFinder respects profiles that BattleMetrics makes private or unavailable and does not attempt to reveal them.
