1About BMFinder

BMFinder is an independent browser extension designed to help Arma 3 players find, recognise, and reconnect with friends, teammates, and other familiar players using information displayed through BattleMetrics.

BMFinder is intended primarily to answer ordinary social questions such as:

  • Are any of my saved friends or teammates playing?
  • Which server are they currently playing on?
  • Has someone changed their in-game name?
  • When did BMFinder last observe a saved player?
  • Which familiar players have recently appeared on servers I follow?

BMFinder is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or produced by BattleMetrics, LLC, Bohemia Interactive, Valve Corporation, or Steam.

“BattleMetrics,” “Arma,” “Arma 3,” “Steam,” and related names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

2Privacy summary

BMFinder is designed to operate locally inside the user’s browser.

BMFinder does not require a BMFinder account. It does not operate a developer-controlled server for collecting player records, and it does not send the user’s saved player information, server history, labels, tags, or activity archive to the developer.

Information collected by BMFinder is stored in the user’s browser profile unless the user deliberately exports it.

BMFinder uses BattleMetrics pages that the user’s browser is already authorised to access. It does not ask the user to provide a BattleMetrics password, authentication token, session cookie, or API key to the developer.

3Information BMFinder may process

Depending on the features used, BMFinder may process and store:

  • BattleMetrics player IDs.
  • Current and previously observed in-game names.
  • User-created nicknames or labels.
  • User-created relationship categories, tags, notes, and favourites.
  • BattleMetrics server IDs, names, game identifiers, and links.
  • Whether a saved player was observed on a followed server.
  • The date and time of an observation.
  • The server on which a player was observed.
  • Server roster snapshots created through an intentional refresh or live-update feature.
  • Imported BattleMetrics player links found in browser bookmarks or a bookmarks file.
  • Extension settings, including appearance, retention, and privacy-blur preferences.
  • Locally generated backup and export data.

BMFinder may read relevant content from BattleMetrics pages rendered in the browser. Passive page reading records information only where required for BMFinder’s disclosed functionality, such as updating a player or server the user has already chosen to save.

BMFinder does not intentionally collect payment information, private messages, Steam passwords, BattleMetrics passwords, authentication cookies, or unrelated browsing history.

4How information is used

BMFinder processes information only to provide its user-facing features, including:

  • Finding players by name or known identifier.
  • Maintaining a locally saved list of friends, teammates, and familiar players.
  • Showing current or recently observed server activity.
  • Helping the user recognise players who change their in-game names.
  • Refreshing saved player and server information.
  • Displaying recent observations and server snapshots.
  • Importing and exporting user-selected information.
  • Creating and restoring local backups.
  • Maintaining extension preferences.
  • Diagnosing extraction failures and displaying relevant warnings.

BMFinder does not use collected information for advertising, behavioural advertising, credit decisions, employment decisions, data brokerage, or sale to third parties.

5Local storage and developer access

BMFinder stores its working data in storage associated with the user’s browser profile.

The developer does not receive, inspect, or have remote access to this locally stored information.

Removing BMFinder may not automatically erase every browser storage entry in all Chromium-based browsers. Users should use BMFinder’s deletion controls before uninstalling when they want to ensure that saved data has been removed.

Clearing the relevant browser profile or extension storage may permanently erase BMFinder data.

6Data retention

BMFinder retains saved friends, players, servers, tags, labels, and settings until the user removes them or clears the extension’s data.

Time-based roster or activity history is retained only for the period selected in BMFinder’s settings. The default retention period is 14 days.

When information falls outside the selected retention period, BMFinder automatically removes it from the active local archive.

Exports and backups created by the user are ordinary files outside BMFinder’s direct control. BMFinder cannot automatically delete copies that the user has exported, shared, duplicated, or backed up elsewhere.

7User controls

BMFinder provides or intends to provide controls that allow users to:

  • Remove an individual saved player.
  • Remove a saved server.
  • Delete history associated with an individual player.
  • Delete history associated with an individual server.
  • Clear roster and activity history.
  • Change the history-retention period.
  • Delete all locally stored BMFinder data.
  • Export a backup.
  • Restore a backup.
  • Enable a privacy blur before taking screenshots.
  • Choose whether player IDs are included in an export.

Users are responsible for protecting any exported files and deleting copies they no longer need.

8Exports and sharing

BMFinder may allow users to export player or server information so that it can be backed up or deliberately shared with another person.

An export may contain identifiers, names, labels, tags, server information, or activity observations.

Users must review the selected export options before creating or sharing a file.

Users should share only the minimum information needed for the intended purpose.

Player IDs should be excluded unless they are genuinely needed, such as when transferring a voluntarily maintained friends list to another person who plays with substantially the same group.

Exported information must not be:

  • Published as a public player database.
  • Sold, licensed, or supplied to a data broker.
  • Used to encourage harassment or unwanted contact.
  • Used to expose a person’s real-world identity.
  • Used to construct or publish detailed behavioural profiles.
  • Used to organise targeted disruption of another person’s gameplay.
  • Shared with someone the user reasonably believes will misuse it.

The person receiving an export must independently obtain and use BMFinder lawfully and responsibly.

9Private and unavailable profiles

BMFinder is designed to respect profile-visibility decisions enforced by BattleMetrics.

When BattleMetrics returns a profile as private, unavailable, or not found, BMFinder must not attempt to defeat that restriction.

BMFinder may display a neutral status such as:

“This profile is private, unavailable, deleted, or no longer accessible.”

BMFinder should not claim that an unavailable profile belongs to a particular person, administrator, moderator, or hidden player.

The absence of a name from a visible roster is not proof that a particular person is present, is using a hidden identity, is acting as an administrator, or has deliberately concealed themselves.

Users must not use roster differences, missing names, or unavailable profiles as the sole basis for accusing or targeting another person.

10Third-party services

BMFinder interacts with pages and information provided by third-party services, principally BattleMetrics.

Use of BattleMetrics remains governed by BattleMetrics’ own terms, privacy policy, access rules, account permissions, and technical restrictions.

BMFinder is designed to use pages that the user is already permitted to access through their own browser session. Users must not use BMFinder to obtain access they would not otherwise possess.

BMFinder does not grant any licence or permission to reproduce, republish, commercially exploit, or publicly distribute content belonging to BattleMetrics or another third party.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of BMFinder and any information obtained through it complies with:

  • BattleMetrics’ applicable terms and policies.
  • The rules of the relevant game server or community.
  • The Chrome Web Store or other extension-distribution platform’s rules.
  • Applicable privacy, data-protection, harassment, computer-misuse, and intellectual-property laws.

BMFinder is designed to respect BattleMetrics’ access controls, but no statement in this policy should be interpreted as a representation that BattleMetrics has approved or endorsed BMFinder.

11Chrome Web Store data-use commitment

BMFinder uses browser permissions and page information only where necessary to provide its clearly disclosed, user-facing purpose.

BMFinder does not sell user data.

BMFinder does not use user data for personalised advertising.

BMFinder does not transfer user data to data brokers, advertising platforms, or unrelated third parties.

BMFinder’s use of data is intended to adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

12Acceptable use

BMFinder may be used to:

  • Save friends, teammates, and familiar community members.
  • Recognise players who have changed their in-game name.
  • Check whether saved players are currently or recently active.
  • Find a server where friends are playing.
  • Maintain personal labels and tags.
  • Share a deliberately selected friends list with a trusted person who participates in the same community.
  • Maintain reasonable personal backups.
  • Assist legitimate server moderation where the user is authorised by the server operator and follows the community’s rules.

Use of BMFinder must remain proportionate to the user’s legitimate social or community-management purpose.

13Prohibited use

Users must not use BMFinder to:

  • Stalk, threaten, bully, intimidate, or harass another person.
  • Persistently monitor someone after being asked to stop.
  • Facilitate unwanted contact across servers or platforms.
  • Dox, identify, or attempt to identify someone in the real world.
  • Discover or expose private contact information.
  • Circumvent a private, unavailable, or access-restricted BattleMetrics profile.
  • Infer and publicly reveal the identity of a player whose profile or session is hidden.
  • Treat a missing roster entry as proof of identity or misconduct.
  • Create a public or commercial database of player activity.
  • Sell, trade, monetise, or broker player data.
  • Coordinate stream sniping, server stalking, raids, disruption, or targeted harassment.
  • Discriminate against a person based on a protected characteristic.
  • Make disciplinary, reputational, or accusatory claims based solely on incomplete BMFinder observations.
  • Use automated activity at a volume intended to evade rate limits, anti-bot systems, or technical protections.
  • Misrepresent BMFinder as an official BattleMetrics, Steam, Valve, Bohemia Interactive, or Arma product.
  • Use BMFinder for any unlawful purpose.
  • Encourage another person to perform any prohibited activity.

14Responsible use

BMFinder is a general-purpose social and informational tool. It is not designed, marketed, or represented as an anti-cheat, moderation, investigative, or enforcement service.

Information made available through BMFinder may be incomplete, delayed, ambiguous, or otherwise affected by factors such as:

  • The extension not running.
  • The browser being closed.
  • A server not being checked.
  • A player changing names.
  • Delays or inaccuracies in third-party services such as BattleMetrics.
  • Hidden player lists.
  • Private sessions or profiles.
  • Extraction, processing, or technical errors.
  • Differences between publicly available information and information available through authorised or administrative systems.

Information provided by BMFinder should therefore be treated as informational context rather than definitive evidence or proof of a person’s actions, intentions, identity, or conduct.

Users remain responsible for how they interpret and use information obtained through BMFinder. This applies equally to individual players, server owners, developers, moderators, administrators, community staff, and any other person or organisation using the service.

BMFinder does not endorse or recommend relying on the service as the sole basis for moderation decisions, disciplinary action, accusations, enforcement measures, reputational claims, or other decisions that may materially affect another person. Where BMFinder information is considered in such a context, users should apply their own independent judgement, verification processes, and appropriate review or appeal procedures.

Any use of BMFinder must comply with our Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Acceptable Use Policy. BMFinder must not be used to harass, threaten, stalk, intimidate, impersonate, target, exploit, or otherwise cause harm to another person, or for any other malicious, abusive, unlawful, or unauthorised purpose.

The responsibility for decisions or actions taken using information obtained through BMFinder remains with the person or organisation making those decisions.
