About BMFinder

BMFinder is a social tool for Arma 3 players: it helps you save and recognise friends and other familiar players you have already crossed paths with, using information already displayed on BattleMetrics.

Independence

“BMFinder is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or produced by BattleMetrics, Valve, Steam, Bohemia Interactive, or Arma.”

What it does

BMFinder runs inside your browser alongside BattleMetrics. It lets you keep a personal, local list of friends, teammates, and other familiar players, then tells you when they are online, which server they are on, and whether they have changed their in-game name since you last saw them. Everything it tracks stays on your own device unless you deliberately export it.

What’s new

Every change that affects what you see or do in BMFinder, newest first.

2.0.2

18/08/2026 Current

Added

  • This list. You can now see what changed in each update without leaving the extension.

Fixed

  • Searching for a quiet server could still miss it. Results came back ordered by how busy each server was, so a near-empty one could be absent altogether rather than merely far down. BMFinder now asks for them ordered by name, narrows the search to the part that tells similar servers apart, and says plainly when it still has not found what you asked for instead of offering near-misses as though they were it.
  • The extractor date on this page read as 2026-07-23, which is easy to misread as a month and a day. It now reads 23/07/2026, like every other date here.

2.0.1

18/08/2026

Added

  • Export people and servers separately. Each has its own tick box in the export window. If you want to share the servers you play on without handing over your list of people, you can now do exactly that, in either file format.
  • Advice on getting better search results, on the Find players screen. It explains why following your servers first makes the difference, and why a shorter search often finds more.
  • Search results now show how well each name matched, as a percentage, so a close guess is distinguishable from an exact hit.

Improved

  • Presentation mode now blurs the labels you gave people, not just their in-game names, and does so on every screen. It was only covering part of the People tab, which made the Live activity screen impossible to share safely.
  • Server search results are ordered by how well the name matches what you typed, rather than by how busy the server is.

Fixed

  • Searching for a server by name could miss it entirely. If you typed the parts of the name you remembered, such as the community and the number, and the real name had other words in between, nothing came back. The search now looks for your words anywhere in the name.
  • Sharing only your servers warned you about player information. The warning appeared even though no player details were in the file.
  • Server links in an activity export pointed at the wrong place for anything that was not an Arma 3 server, so the link did not open.
  • The export button no longer waits for you to press it to tell you nothing is selected. It is simply unavailable until there is something to export.

2.0.0

14/08/2026 First public release

Added

  • Choose what comes back from a backup. Importing a file lets you pick which parts to restore — people, servers, activity, settings — and either add to what you have or replace it.
  • Choose what leaves your machine. The export window asks what the file is for and lets you include or leave out individual details.
  • Presentation mode, which keeps names and player IDs blurred even when the mouse passes over them, for screen sharing.
  • A first-run explanation of what the extension reads and stores, which you have to accept before anything is recorded.

Improved

  • Importing and exporting now live together under Data and privacy, instead of being spread across several screens.
  • Clearer wording throughout. People you have saved are described as saved people rather than as a watchlist.

Fixed

  • The menu on each person did nothing. Every option in it — renaming, tags, notes, copying an ID, removing someone — failed silently, in both the tile and the list view.
  • Managing tags and viewing previous names failed in the tile view specifically.
  • Long dates were cut off in the summary boxes on the Data and privacy screen.
