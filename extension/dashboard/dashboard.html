Observation-based results

BMFinder only knows about checks that occurred while it was running. A missing observation does not mean someone was offline — private sessions, hidden server rosters, delayed information, name changes, or a failed check can all leave a gap.

Live updates

Each round loads every followed server once and stores who was on it. Cycles are scheduled only while this dashboard is open; your choice of interval and whether live updates were was on are remembered, so closing the tab pauses the work rather than losing the setup. Pause stops scheduling new cycles and lets any cycle in flight finish. Stop also cancels the cycle in flight. Neither deletes anything.

Retention

Retention applies to recent activity and server-check history. Saved people, labels, tags, and followed servers remain until you remove them.

Controls

Every control below states what it removes before it runs.

Clears stored snapshots and presence history collected so far, the same records retention prunes automatically. Saved people, labels, tags and followed servers are kept.

Removes every saved person from your saved people, including their label, note, tag assignments and recorded name history. Observations already recorded on your followed servers are not removed by this — they stay until retention prunes them or you clear recent activity. You can undo the removal itself immediately afterwards.

Removes every followed server from your list. Snapshots and presence already recorded while you followed it are not removed by this — they stay until retention prunes them or you clear recent activity. Saved people and their history are not affected.

Clears every saved person's label, note and tag assignments. Relationships are kept. The tag catalogue itself is kept too: your tags stay defined, but nothing stays tagged.
