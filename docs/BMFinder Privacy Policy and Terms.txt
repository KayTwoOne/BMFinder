1. About BMFinder
BMFinder is an independent browser extension designed to help Arma 3 players find, recognise, and
reconnect with friends, teammates, and other familiar players using information displayed through
BattleMetrics.
BMFinder is intended primarily to answer ordinary social questions such as:
Are any of my saved friends or teammates playing?
Which server are they currently playing on?
Has someone changed their in-game name?
When did BMFinder last observe a saved player?
Which familiar players have recently appeared on servers I follow?
BMFinder is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or produced by BattleMetrics, LLC, Bohemia
Interactive, Valve Corporation, or Steam.
“BattleMetrics,” “Arma,” “Arma 3,” “Steam,” and related names and trademarks belong to their respective
owners.
2. Privacy summary
BMFinder is designed to operate locally inside the user’s browser.
BMFinder does not require a BMFinder account. It does not operate a developer-controlled server for
collecting player records, and it does not send the user’s saved player information, server history,
labels, tags, or activity archive to the developer.
Information collected by BMFinder is stored in the user’s browser profile unless the user deliberately
exports it.
BMFinder uses BattleMetrics pages that the user’s browser is already authorised to access. It does not
ask the user to provide a BattleMetrics password, authentication token, session cookie, or API key to the
developer.

3. Information BMFinder may process
Depending on the features used, BMFinder may process and store:
BattleMetrics player IDs.
Current and previously observed in-game names.
User-created nicknames or labels.
User-created relationship categories, tags, notes, and favourites.
BattleMetrics server IDs, names, game identifiers, and links.
Whether a saved player was observed on a followed server.
The date and time of an observation.
The server on which a player was observed.
Server roster snapshots created through an intentional refresh or live-update feature.
Imported BattleMetrics player links found in browser bookmarks or a bookmarks file.
Extension settings, including appearance, retention, and privacy-blur preferences.
Locally generated backup and export data.
BMFinder may read relevant content from BattleMetrics pages rendered in the browser. Passive page
reading records information only where required for BMFinder’s disclosed functionality, such as
updating a player or server the user has already chosen to save.
BMFinder does not intentionally collect payment information, private messages, Steam passwords,
BattleMetrics passwords, authentication cookies, or unrelated browsing history.
4. How information is used
BMFinder processes information only to provide its user-facing features, including:
Finding players by name or known identifier.
Maintaining a locally saved list of friends, teammates, and familiar players.
Showing current or recently observed server activity.
Helping the user recognise players who change their in-game names.
Refreshing saved player and server information.
Displaying recent observations and server snapshots.
Importing and exporting user-selected information.
Creating and restoring local backups.
Maintaining extension preferences.
Diagnosing extraction failures and displaying relevant warnings.
BMFinder does not use collected information for advertising, behavioural advertising, credit decisions,
employment decisions, data brokerage, or sale to third parties.
5. Local storage and developer access
BMFinder stores its working data in storage associated with the user’s browser profile.
The developer does not receive, inspect, or have remote access to this locally stored information.

Removing BMFinder may not automatically erase every browser storage entry in all Chromium-based
browsers. Users should use BMFinder’s deletion controls before uninstalling when they want to ensure
that saved data has been removed.
Clearing the relevant browser profile or extension storage may permanently erase BMFinder data.
6. Data retention
BMFinder retains saved friends, players, servers, tags, labels, and settings until the user removes them
or clears the extension’s data.
Time-based roster or activity history is retained only for the period selected in BMFinder’s settings. The
default retention period is [14 days].
When information falls outside the selected retention period, BMFinder automatically removes it from
the active local archive.
Exports and backups created by the user are ordinary files outside BMFinder’s direct control. BMFinder
cannot automatically delete copies that the user has exported, shared, duplicated, or backed up
elsewhere.
7. User controls
BMFinder provides or intends to provide controls that allow users to:
Remove an individual saved player.
Remove a saved server.
Delete history associated with an individual player.
Delete history associated with an individual server.
Clear roster and activity history.
Change the history-retention period.
Delete all locally stored BMFinder data.
Export a backup.
Restore a backup.
Enable a privacy blur before taking screenshots.
Choose whether player IDs are included in an export.
Users are responsible for protecting any exported files and deleting copies they no longer need.
8. Exports and sharing
BMFinder may allow users to export player or server information so that it can be backed up or
deliberately shared with another person.
An export may contain identifiers, names, labels, tags, server information, or activity observations.
Users must review the selected export options before creating or sharing a file.
Users should share only the minimum information needed for the intended purpose.

Player IDs should be excluded unless they are genuinely needed, such as when transferring a
voluntarily maintained friends list to another person who plays with substantially the same group.
Exported information must not be:
Published as a public player database.
Sold, licensed, or supplied to a data broker.
Used to encourage harassment or unwanted contact.
Used to expose a person’s real-world identity.
Used to construct or publish detailed behavioural profiles.
Used to organise targeted disruption of another person’s gameplay.
Shared with someone the user reasonably believes will misuse it.
The person receiving an export must independently obtain and use BMFinder lawfully and responsibly.
9. Private and unavailable profiles
BMFinder is designed to respect profile-visibility decisions enforced by BattleMetrics.
When BattleMetrics returns a profile as private, unavailable, or not found, BMFinder must not attempt
to defeat that restriction.
BMFinder may display a neutral status such as:
“This profile is private, unavailable, deleted, or no longer accessible.”
BMFinder should not claim that an unavailable profile belongs to a particular person, administrator,
moderator, or hidden player.
The absence of a name from a visible roster is not proof that a particular person is present, is using a
hidden identity, is acting as an administrator, or has deliberately concealed themselves.
Users must not use roster differences, missing names, or unavailable profiles as the sole basis for
accusing or targeting another person.
10. Third-party services
BMFinder interacts with pages and information provided by third-party services, principally
BattleMetrics.
Use of BattleMetrics remains governed by BattleMetrics’ own terms, privacy policy, access rules, account
permissions, and technical restrictions.
BMFinder is designed to use pages that the user is already permitted to access through their own
browser session. Users must not use BMFinder to obtain access they would not otherwise possess.
BMFinder does not grant any licence or permission to reproduce, republish, commercially exploit, or
publicly distribute content belonging to BattleMetrics or another third party.

12. Acceptable use
BMFinder may be used to:
Save friends, teammates, and familiar community members.
Recognise players who have changed their in-game name.
Check whether saved players are currently or recently active.
Find a server where friends are playing.
Maintain personal labels and tags.
Share a deliberately selected friends list with a trusted person who participates in the same
community.
Maintain reasonable personal backups.
Assist legitimate server moderation where the user is authorised by the server operator and
follows the community’s rules.
Use of BMFinder must remain proportionate to the user’s legitimate social or community-management
purpose.

14. Moderation and anti-cheat use
BMFinder information may be incomplete, delayed, ambiguous, or affected by factors such as:
The extension not running.
The browser being closed.
A server not being checked.
A player changing names.
BattleMetrics delays.
Hidden player lists.
Private sessions or profiles.
Extraction errors.
Differences between public and authorised server-administration views.
BMFinder observations are leads, not proof.
Server staff must not issue a ban, public accusation, punishment, or reputational claim solely because
BMFinder shows—or fails to show—a player at a particular time.
Where BMFinder contributes to a moderation concern, staff should verify it through independent
evidence and follow the server community’s normal review and appeal procedures.
15. Accuracy and availability
BMFinder depends on third-party pages, selectors, services, and information that can change without
notice.
The extension may produce incomplete, outdated, incorrectly matched, or unavailable information.
Users must independently verify important conclusions.
